## Check: where the news-gap gains came from

- 343 gaps in 26 years (about 13 a year), so the equal-weight version often held just 1-2 stocks: 1 open on 14% of days, 2 open on 12%.
- 88 of the gaps came in 2008-09, mostly banks bouncing off the crisis lows (FITB four times, C, BAC, HIG, COF). Those 6-month trades beat SPY by +37% on average.
- In all other years (188 gaps, 2020 excluded), the average 6-month result beat SPY by +11%, with a median of +2.6%; 55% beat SPY. That's a real edge, but a modest one, carried by a few big winners.
- The 10-slot version, which sizes every trade the same, made only +12.5% a year. Most of the account sat in cash for lack of signals.
